* [Perplexity and Intelligence](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/): `GPT‑2 was arguably the first model capable of consistently formulating coherent paragraphs of text. The next major leap, GPT‑3, was capable of learning in context, given a few examples of a task. GPT‑3.5, at a log-perplexity of ~0.99 on our current main evaluation dataset, was capable of fluent conversation and formed the basis for the original ChatGPT. GPT‑4, at ~0.91, was proficient at utilizing chain-of-thought reasoning productively, which motivated the subsequent targeted elicitation of this capability and the creation of modern reasoning models. Now, as we have reached losses below 0.8, we have seen the emergence of transformative capabilities in fields like cybersecurity and mathematics.`
* 这篇很有意思，[Michael Crichton, psychology, and AI](https://austinkleon.com/2026/11/10/michael-crichton-psychology-and-ai/)，`the Eliza Effect, or people’s desire to make the computer seem smarter than it really is.` 具体来说是 `They wanted so much to believe that it was a thinking creature that they would ask it questions in a way that, with practice, they knew would cause it to give the most humanlike response… And again, that was one of the early things I learned, that it’s not just that we come to a machine and say: “Oh, is this intelligent? Does this understand me?” But we come to these conversations wanting it to be there for us.` 人类啊，真是太有趣了，主动迎合机器的逻辑，还是太渴望被倾听了。
